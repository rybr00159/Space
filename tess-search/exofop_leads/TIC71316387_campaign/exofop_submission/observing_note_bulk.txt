CTOI 71316387.01 is an EB at 2x the CTOI period, not a planet: P = 1.30367228 +/- 0.00000019 d, primary T0 = BJD_TDB 2460165.67042 +/- 0.00018 (TESS-SPOC S1/28/68, TESScut S95/105/106); the CTOI epoch is a secondary eclipse. Depths 5770/2600 ppm, deeper on the same parity in all six sectors. The G star is not in the EB: its Gaia DR3 RV is constant (15 RVS transits over 946 d, chi2 p = 0.28), vs K of 18 km/s or more for any stellar EB partner. Colour-corrected difference-image centroids put the source within 1.24 arcsec of the G star (95%); all Gaia neighbours (18 arcsec and beyond) excluded. The TESS centroid colour shift and SuperWASP/NEOWISE W1 depths show a red (early-M, possibly late-K) eclipsed star; the eclipse shape with MS relations gives an ~M2V + ~M4V pair with ~2.6% of TESS flux (not dynamical). Likely a bound triple (chance alignment disfavoured ~5000-14000x for baseline priors, ~200x worst case), unconfirmed.
